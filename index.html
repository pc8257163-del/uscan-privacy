# uscan-privacy Uscan Privacy Policy

Uscan Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

1. Overview

Uscan ("the app") is a QR code and barcode scanner developed by YOUR_NAME ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. The app has no user accounts and no server of its own.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or precise location. The content of the codes you scan is never uploaded to us.

3. Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • Your scan history (the content of scanned codes, their type and the time of the scan)
  • Your favorites
  • Your settings (sound, vibration, theme, auto-open links, save to history)
  • The end time of the optional 24-hour "remove ads" reward

This data stays on your phone. You can delete your history inside the app, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

4. Camera and photos

The app uses the camera only to scan codes. Camera frames are analysed on your device in real time and are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere by us. If you choose an image from your gallery to scan, the app reads that image on your device to find a code.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan QR codes and barcodes.
  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to open links you choose.
  • The app may use your phone's vibration for scan feedback.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In regions where the law requires it (for example the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before using this information for personalized ads.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings, then Google, then Ads), and you can reopen your consent choices from the app's Settings where available. Watching the optional rewarded video hides banner ads for 24 hours.

7. Links and other apps

When you open a scanned link, send an email, make a call, connect to Wi-Fi, save a contact or open a map, the app hands the action to your browser or another app, and their own privacy policies apply. Scanned content can be harmful. The app shows a warning for suspicious links but cannot guarantee safety, so please check before you open anything.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

Your scan history and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your device. No method of storage is completely secure, but we only keep what the app needs.

10. Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold your personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to access or delete on your behalf. For data processed by Google, you can use the tools in your Google account settings. If you have any questions, contact us using the email below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with a new effective date.
